# Agent-proof acceptance-criteria review — SlotBook PRD

## Verdict

The PRD is in good shape for agent use. Nearly every FR has observable consequences,
the cross-references resolve, and the decisions in `.memlog.md` show up faithfully in
the text. Two problems should be fixed before stories are cut. The capacity model,
meaning what "reserved" counts and how a practitioner booking uses a place, is
defined in two incompatible ways. And FR-14 literally exempts practitioner bookings
from the capacity and closed-slot rules. Most other findings are undefined
boundaries: ticket codes reused across final bookings, sequence numbers after a
cancellation, a "patient name" field the glossary doesn't have, and owner alerts with
no channel. Each story's agent would fill these in differently.

Counts: **2 critical, 10 high, 24 medium, 12 low.**

Line numbers are approximate and refer to `prd-slotbook.md` as of this review.

---

## Critical

- **[critical] Two incompatible meanings of "reserved"** (§3 *Place* L160–161 vs §3 *Release* L164; FR-10 L354; FR-13 L393–401; FR-15 L440–443; FR-18 L497–498; FR-40 L830; UJ-2 L82–86; UJ-5 L107) — Reading A comes from the glossary. A place is exactly one of *free*, *reserved* or *taken*. So a practitioner booking into a reserved place turns it into *taken*, and the reserved count drops by one. Reading B comes from the glossary's own *Release* entry, FR-13 and UJ-2, which speak of "**unused** reserved places". UJ-2 has 3 reserved places, of which "2 … are still unused", so the reserved count includes places already booked. The FRs that do arithmetic disagree depending on the reading:
  - Under B, FR-10's floor "taken + reserved" counts a used reserved place twice, and FR-40's *free* comes out wrong.
  - Under A, FR-18's "reserved again" has to increment `reserved`.
  - FR-15's "taken places never exceed *capacity − reserved* for patient bookings" reads both as "patient-made bookings only" and as "all taken places". The first reading lets patients overbook once a practitioner has booked into a free place.
  - FR-9 doesn't say whether patients see "3 reserved" or "2 reserved" after a practitioner books into one.

  It is also unstated whether the practitioner chooses *reserved* or *free* when booking (UJ-5 "opens a reserved place"), or whether the system picks. FR-18 needs to know which was used. FR-15 (epic 4) and FR-12 to FR-14 (epic 6) are separate stories, so they will pick different readings. *Fix:* define it once in §3: `reserved` counts only **unused** reserved places, and `free = capacity − taken − reserved`, where `taken` includes every non-cancelled booking. Also state:
  - a patient booking needs `free ≥ 1`;
  - a practitioner booking states explicitly whether it uses a reserved or a free place, moves one place from that pool to `taken`, and records which pool it used;
  - rewrite FR-13, the *Release* entry and UJ-2 to match.
- **[critical] FR-14 says a practitioner booking is "refused only if" there is a duplicate** (FR-14 L414–416) — "it is refused only if the patient already has a booking in the same slot". If an agent builds the story from FR-14's bullets alone and follows them literally, practitioner bookings will be allowed into a **closed** slot (against FR-11 L365, "no new booking of any kind") and into a slot with no free and no reserved place (against FR-15's "*capacity* in total"). *Fix:* say "it is **not** refused for the booking limit; it is still refused if the slot is closed, if it has no place of the requested kind (`slot_full`), or if the patient already has a booking in the slot".

## High

- **[high] Sequence numbers contradict themselves after a cancellation** (§3 *Sequence number* L179–180; FR-15 L445–446; FR-10 L354) — The glossary says "1…capacity, given in booking order", and FR-15 says "the next sequence number". In a 12-place slot where #5 cancels, the 13th booking either gets 13, which is outside 1…capacity, or reuses 5, which is not booking order and breaks walk-in queue order. Lowering capacity (FR-10) can also leave issued numbers above the new capacity. Nothing requires two concurrent bookings to get different numbers either, and NFR-1 doesn't list sequence numbers. *Fix:* pick one rule, either "monotonic per slot, never reused, may exceed capacity" or "lowest unused number in 1…capacity". Add "concurrent bookings in one slot never share a number", with a test.
- **[high] The ticket alphabet has 31 symbols, not 27** (FR-22 L552–554; addendum *Ticket code* "about 531k" = 27⁴) — `A–Z` without `O`, `I`, `L` is 23 letters, and `2–9` is 8 digits, which makes 31 symbols (31⁴ ≈ 924k). An agent will either implement the listed alphabet and fail a "27 symbols" assertion, or invent 4 more exclusions. *Fix:* either change the count to 31 (and ≈924k in the addendum), or list the exact 27 characters.
- **[high] Door-check lookup is undefined once codes are reused, and result precedence is not stated** (FR-22 L555–556; FR-24 L578–585; §3 *Active booking* L170) — FR-22 allows codes of final bookings to be reused. But FR-24 must return *Already checked in*, *Cancelled* and *Lapsed*, which are results about final bookings. So one code can match a `confirmed` booking and one or more older final bookings at the same time. "No such active **or recent** ticket" never defines *recent*. There is also no precedence among the results. A cancelled booking for tomorrow could be *Not today* or *Cancelled*, and a booking checked in yesterday could be *Not today* or *Already checked in*. The uniqueness rule uses "active", which the glossary defines by time (slot not ended). A partial unique index can't express that, so an agent will use state instead. *Fix:* make uniqueness apply to `confirmed` bookings. The lookup returns the `confirmed` booking if there is one, otherwise the most recent final booking within N days, otherwise *Not genuine*. Give a fixed evaluation order for the results.
- **[high] The public check has no answer for `checked_in` or `lapsed`** (FR-26 L611–613) — It "answers only **Genuine for ⟨date⟩**, **Cancelled** or **Not genuine**". A used (checked-in) or lapsed ticket, the classic resold ticket from UJ-7, could map to *Genuine for ⟨date⟩* (the code is real) or to *Not genuine*. A `confirmed` booking for a past date is just as unclear. *Fix:* add a state-to-answer table: `confirmed` gives Genuine for ⟨date⟩; `cancelled` gives Cancelled; `checked_in` and `lapsed` give, for example, Not genuine or a fourth "Already used / expired" answer.
- **[high] SM-2 asks for checks that FR-24 and FR-26 can't perform** (SM-2 L1029–1031) — SM-2 says "wrong-number tickets are rejected **at the door check**", but the door check takes no mobile number (FR-24; UJ-4 compares the digits by hand). It also says "wrong-day tickets are rejected **at the public check**", but FR-26 answers *Genuine for ⟨date⟩* for any day. A test-writing agent will hit cases that can't be implemented. *Fix:* split SM-2 per surface. The door check rejects forged, reused, wrong-day and cancelled codes. The public check rejects forged, wrong-number and cancelled codes, and shows the date.
- **[high] "Patient's name" is used, but a patient has no name** (§3 *Patient* L143–144 and *Attendee* L171–172 vs FR-11 L367 "patient name"; FR-35 L762 "the patient's name"; FR-41 L835–838 "find a patient by … name", "the patient's name") — Names exist only on attendees, per booking, and a patient can book for several family members. UJ-8 has a patient chatting before any booking, so that patient has no name anywhere. One agent will add a `Patient.Name` column; another will derive the name from attendees. *Fix:* replace "patient name" with "attendee name" in FR-11. Define FR-41's name search as matching any attendee name on the patient's bookings. State what FR-35 shows when there is no booking, for example the last attendee name used, or else only the masked number.
- **[high] "The owner is alerted", but there is no alert channel** (FR-3 L256; FR-27 L642–643 "visible to the owner"; NFR-2 L913 "reports any drift to the owner"; NFR-17 L966) — The glossary defines *Notification* as a message to a **patient**, there is no email in v1, and no FR describes a staff alert surface. Four consequences have no observable pass/fail, and agents will invent different mechanisms: SMS to the owner, a banner, a log line. *Fix:* add one consequence, for example in FR-40, saying where an owner alert appears (for instance, a list on the owner's dashboard that stays until acknowledged). State that this is the only alert channel in v1, and point FR-3, FR-27, NFR-2 and NFR-17 to it.
- **[high] The reason-category/note restriction conflicts with views open to all practitioners** (FR-16 L470–472 vs FR-35 L762, FR-41 L838, FR-40 L823–829) — FR-16 limits reason category and note to "the patient, the practitioner **of the slot** and owners". But any practitioner can open any conversation and see "active bookings" (FR-35), and can look up any patient and see "their bookings (all states)" (FR-41). Neither says which booking fields are shown. FR-40 only implies that a practitioner's day view covers their own slots ("An owner can see every practitioner's day"). These are health-adjacent fields (NFR-11a). *Fix:* list the booking fields shown in FR-35 and FR-41, and state that reason category and note are left out for bookings in another practitioner's slots. In FR-40, state that a practitioner's day view covers only their own slots.
- **[high] "At most one booking in the same slot" doesn't say whether cancelled bookings count** (FR-15 L444; FR-14 L415–416) — A natural unique index on (slot, patient) stops a patient rebooking a slot they cancelled earlier, for example after cancelling by mistake. "Hold" in FR-15 and "has a booking" in FR-14 support both readings. *Fix:* say "at most one non-cancelled booking per patient per slot; a cancelled booking never blocks a new booking in the same slot".
- **[high] Draft-week copy: UJ-1 and FR-7 describe different behaviour** (UJ-1 L73–75 vs FR-7 L315–316) — In UJ-1 the draft is "already there" and "the **empty days** were copied", which suggests an automatic, per-day copy. FR-7 says a draft "**can be** pre-filled", which suggests a user action, and "never overwrites slots", which suggests a per-slot copy. It is unspecified:
  - who triggers the copy (a practitioner for their own slots, or the owner for everyone);
  - what happens when a copied slot partly overlaps an existing one, which would break FR-7's no-overlap rule: skip it, fail the whole copy, or copy per empty day;
  - whether reserved counts and closed status are copied;
  - whether "shifted by 7 days" keeps the local time or adds 168 hours in UTC (these differ across a DST change).

  *Fix:* state the trigger and who may use it. Make the copy per practitioner per empty day, matching UJ-1, or per slot with overlapping copies skipped and listed. Say that only time and capacity are copied (no reserved count, no closed status), and that the local date and time are shifted by 7 days.

## Medium

- **[medium] "Active booking" depends on the clock, but enforcement depends on state** (§3 L170; FR-17 L479; FR-22 L555; FR-21 L532–533; NFR-1 L907) — An active booking is "`confirmed` whose slot has not ended", but lapsing takes up to 15 minutes. A commit-time guard (counter row or partial index, as NFR-1 and the addendum suggest) will count by state. A clock-based check won't. For up to 15 minutes after a slot ends, the two give different `limit_reached` results. *Fix:* either define *active* as "state `confirmed`" and accept the lapse lag, or require the limit guard to ignore bookings whose slot has ended.
- **[medium] The list of FR-15 failure reasons is incomplete** (FR-15 L436–439; FR-14 L417–419) — It presents `slot_full | slot_closed | slot_started | limit_reached` as the full set, but there is no code for a duplicate in the same slot, a FR-16 validation failure, an unknown or draft-week slot, or a bad idempotency key. The "reserved means reserved" test doesn't say what the refusal looks like either, since a patient books a slot, not a place. *Fix:* list every code (for example `already_booked`, `validation_failed`, `not_found`). Restate the FR-14 test as: a slot with 0 free and ≥1 reserved places returns `slot_full` to a patient session, and no request field can target a reserved place.
- **[medium] Practitioner booking into a draft week or a started slot** (FR-14 L403–416; FR-12 L384 allows reserving in a draft week; FR-7 L317) — FR-14 doesn't limit practitioner bookings to published, not-yet-started slots. A booking in a draft week would send a ticket and an SMS that reveal an unpublished week. Booking a late arrival into a 09:00–11:00 walk-in window at 09:30 may be intended, but it isn't stated either way. *Fix:* state whether practitioner bookings need a published week, and whether they are allowed after the slot starts (for example, until it ends).
- **[medium] Scope of the known-device skip and session lifetime** (§3 *Known device* L149–150; FR-4 L262–274; FR-5 L285; FR-32 L705) — The skip is defined for "a booking". It doesn't say whether, once the session has expired, a known device can also open My bookings, chat or the fraud form without an OTP. FR-32's "(OTP)" could mean "any patient session" or "a fresh OTP". It also doesn't say whether the 30-day session is absolute or extends with use. "Sign out everywhere" appears only as a parenthesis in FR-4, so it is unclear whether it is a feature to build. *Fix:* state where the known-device skip applies, whether FR-32 accepts a known-device session, whether the session lifetime is absolute or sliding, and add "sign out everywhere" as a FR-5 consequence (or remove it).
- **[medium] Fraud report: public or signed-in?** (FR-32 L699, L705 vs NFR-6 L924–925 vs UJ-7 L119–123) — FR-32 requires a patient session. NFR-6 lists "fraud report" among **public** endpoints, and UJ-7 goes from the public check page straight to filing, without sign-in. *Fix:* add an OTP step to UJ-7, and in NFR-6 call the fraud report "session-required, also rate-limited per IP", or drop it from that list.
- **[medium] The FR-39 settings list is missing country, and editing the time zone is unsafe** (FR-1 L220; §3 *Week* L151–152; FR-39 L811–815) — FR-1 calls the country a tenant setting, but FR-39 doesn't list it. The time zone *is* listed as owner-editable, yet week boundaries, "today", the daily OTP budget and stored slot times all depend on it, while the week start day is fixed at setup. *Fix:* mark country, time zone and week start day as setup-only (not editable in v1), or state what a time-zone change does to existing slots and bookings.
- **[medium] Settings have no valid ranges** (FR-17 L479–481; FR-29 L663–664; FR-3 L253–257; FR-22 L553–554; FR-34 L749–750) — Only the cut-off has a minimum. It isn't stated whether max active can be 0, whether the weekly limit can be below the active limit, what the reminder lead time can range over and how "off" is represented, how long the prefix can be and which characters it allows, or whether the response time is a number or free text. *Fix:* give a minimum, a maximum and a type for each FR-39 setting.
- **[medium] What a change to reminder lead time or ticket prefix does** (FR-39 L816–817; FR-29; FR-22 L553–558) — Only limits and the cut-off are covered. It isn't stated whether already-scheduled reminders move, whether active codes keep the old prefix, whether a code typed with the old prefix still matches, or whether the hyphen is optional when typed (`SB4K7P`). *Fix:* add one consequence for each: reminders are recomputed (or not), and the prefix is display-only (or stored with each code), with the matching rule.
- **[medium] Owner-entered text and the three languages** (FR-43 L870–871 vs FR-16 reason categories, FR-31 warning "with a default text", FR-30 labels, FR-34 response time, FR-33/FR-36 "text per language") — "Every patient-facing string in `bn`/`en`/`ar`; a missing translation fails the build" can't cover owner-entered text. It is unstated which owner fields have a version per language, and what is shown when the active language has none. *Fix:* list the owner-entered fields that have a version per language, give the fallback (for example, the tenant's default language), and limit the build check to built-in strings.
- **[medium] Unit for text-length limits** (FR-16 L458, L462; FR-20 L521; FR-32 L702; FR-33 L722; FR-34 L746) — "1–100 characters, any script" gives different results in UTF-16 units, code points and grapheme clusters for Bangla and Arabic. The frontend and backend stories may each pick a different unit. *Fix:* name one unit (for example, grapheme clusters as in the addendum's *Text* note, or code points after NFC) and apply it on both sides.
- **[medium] Which digit script is used** (FR-43 L872–879; FR-1) — "Formatted for the active language" could produce Bengali or Arabic-Indic digits for times, dates, sequence numbers and counts. It isn't stated whether ticket codes, mobile numbers and OTPs use Western digits, or whether input in Bengali digits is accepted. *Fix:* state the digit script for each kind of value, and that input in native digits is normalised.
- **[medium] Order of My bookings, and a missing group** (FR-19 L509–510) — "Newest first" could mean by slot date or by creation time, and it isn't stated whether it applies to the active group. A `confirmed` booking whose slot has ended but hasn't lapsed yet is neither active nor in the listed past states. *Fix:* order active bookings by slot start ascending and past bookings by slot start descending (or similar), and say that a booking whose slot has ended belongs to the past group.
- **[medium] Side effects of FR-44 deletion** (FR-44 L889–901) — It isn't stated whether the patient's sessions and known-device markers are revoked. A marker bound to the number could otherwise skip the OTP for the "new patient". It also isn't stated whether the cancelled bookings send a cancellation SMS, given the number is removed and sending is asynchronous, or who is recorded as the actor and reason. *Fix:* add: all sessions and markers are revoked; cancellations send no notification (or the notification is sent before the number is removed); actor = patient, reason = "data deletion".
- **[medium] The FR-42 audit list misses events that other FRs audit** (FR-42 L846–850 vs NFR-9 L935–936 CSV export, FR-44 L900 deletion, FR-38 L802–803 "refused and recorded"; NFR-11a L942) — An agent building the audit story from FR-42 won't cover these. FR-42's exclusion list also leaves out the **reason category**, while NFR-11a keeps it out of logs. *Fix:* add CSV export, data deletion, refused staff actions and publication scheduling to the list, and add the reason category to the exclusions.
- **[medium] FR-11: bulk cancel failures, and what "close a whole date" means** (FR-11 L361–369; reconcile-prior-plan G5) — If one booking in the batch was checked in or cancelled concurrently, the bulk cancel could be all-or-nothing or per item. G5 assumed "already implied", but the FR text doesn't say. "Close a whole date" could mean bulk-closing the existing slots or a date-level closure that also blocks slots added later. *Fix:* say bulk cancel reports a result for each booking and never reports full success when one failed. Define closing a date as closing every existing slot on it (or as a date-level state).
- **[medium] FR-10: editing times or deleting a published slot is neither allowed nor forbidden** (FR-10 L351–357; FR-42 L846 "every slot change after publication") — FR-10 lists add, raise, lower and close. An agent may also add time edits and deletion. *Fix:* add "No other change is allowed: a published slot's date and times can't be edited and it can't be deleted" (or state the rule).
- **[medium] FR-7: touching slots and which weeks can be drafts** (FR-7 L306–317) — It isn't stated whether "can't overlap" allows 09:00–11:00 next to 11:00–12:00, whether only the next week can be a draft or any future week, or whether a draft week has to be created explicitly. *Fix:* use half-open intervals (touching is allowed), and say which weeks can exist as drafts and how a draft comes into being.
- **[medium] Scheduled publication: timing and link to the publication-day setting** (FR-8 L326–329; FR-39 L813) — "At that moment" has no tolerance. It isn't stated whether a schedule can be changed or cancelled, whether slots can be edited after scheduling, or whether the informational "opens on ⟨day⟩ at ⟨time⟩" is linked to the scheduled time or independent of it. *Fix:* give a tolerance (for example, within 60 s), say the schedule can be edited or cancelled until it fires, and state that the two values are independent (or that one is derived from the other).
- **[medium] Availability: public or signed-in, and are closed slots shown?** (FR-9 L334; UJ-3 L91–93; NFR-6 L925) — FR-9 says "a patient", which the glossary defines as a verified number. UJ-3 and NFR-6 imply anyone can see availability before signing in. It isn't stated whether closed or started slots are hidden or shown greyed out. *Fix:* say "anyone, without signing in", and state how closed and started slots are shown.
- **[medium] Idempotency response and "nearest" slots** (FR-15 L447–450) — It isn't stated what a retry with the same key returns (the original booking, or an error), what happens if the same key is used for a different slot, or whether "nearest free slots" means nearest in time to the requested slot or to now, and whether that spans other dates or weeks. *Fix:* a retry returns the original result, a key reused with a different slot is refused, and "nearest" means the next free slots by start time after the requested slot, within published weeks (or whatever is intended).
- **[medium] NFR-6 limits have no values, and "every limit returns 429" is too broad** (NFR-6 L924–926) — No per-IP figures are given for OTP verify or availability. "Every limit returns HTTP 429" could be read to include `limit_reached` (FR-17) and the daily OTP budget. *Fix:* give the missing figures, and limit the 429 rule to rate limits. Business limits use their own error codes.
- **[medium] Shared inbox: unread count and "done"** (FR-35 L760–763) — It isn't stated whether the unread count is per staff user or shared, or how a *done* conversation whose last message is from the patient is sorted. *Fix:* say whether read state is per user or shared, and that "unanswered" means the last message is from the patient whatever the *done* flag says (or the reverse).
- **[medium] What disabling a practitioner does to their slots** (FR-38 L793–804) — It isn't stated whether a disabled practitioner's published slots stay bookable, whether patients still see them, or whether existing bookings are kept. *Fix:* add a consequence, for example "slots and bookings are unchanged; the owner must close or cancel them" (or hidden from patients).
- **[medium] Races between transitions other than double check-in** (FR-21 L535; NFR-1 L907–911) — Cancel against check-in and cancel against lapse aren't required to be atomic, and NFR-1 tests only double check-in. *Fix:* require every transition to apply only if the state is still `confirmed` at commit, with a test that races cancel against check-in.

## Low

- **[low] The ticket's fields differ between the glossary, FR-23 and UJ-4** (§3 *Ticket* L177–178 has no attendee name; FR-23 L565 has it; UJ-4 L101–102 shows "today's date" but no age, and labels the result "Already used at 09:14" where FR-24 says "Already checked in") — *Fix:* align the glossary and UJ-4 with FR-23 and FR-24.
- **[low] The masked-number format is defined only in FR-24** (FR-24 L579 "last 3 digits shown" vs FR-11, FR-35, FR-40, FR-41, NFR-9 "masked") — *Fix:* define *masked mobile number* once in §3.
- **[low] NFR-11a points to FR-16 for showing age, but FR-16 doesn't cover age** (NFR-11a L940–942; FR-16 L470–472; FR-24 L579; FR-40 L825) — *Fix:* add age to FR-16's visibility rule, with the door check and day view as allowed exceptions, or reword NFR-11a.
- **[low] FR-27 says push is "never instead of" SMS, but FR-37 sends chat replies by push only** (FR-27 L646–647 vs FR-37 L777–779). FR-27 L641 also lists check-in among commits that notify, but FR-28 has no check-in notification. *Fix:* limit FR-27's push rule to FR-28, FR-29 and FR-33, and remove check-in from L641 (or add it to FR-28).
- **[low] Rate-limit windows aren't stated** (FR-26 L616; FR-32 L705 "3 a day"; FR-34 L752 "20 an hour") — Rolling or fixed, and for "a day", calendar day in the tenant's time zone or 24 hours. Only FR-3 says "rolling". *Fix:* state the window for each.
- **[low] Door-check "throttled"** (FR-24 L588–589) — This could mean rejecting (429, as in NFR-6) or delaying, and it isn't stated whether there is one audit entry per refused lookup or per episode. *Fix:* "refused with 429; one audit entry per minute of throttling" (or similar).
- **[low] Bullets without a measurable bound** (FR-8 L327 "at that moment"; FR-23 L570 "scans … at normal brightness"; FR-34 L753 and FR-35 L764 "without a page reload", with no latency) — *Fix:* give bounds (for example, "visible within 5 s") or mark them as manual checks.
- **[low] Inclusive or exclusive boundaries** (FR-18 L496–500 cut-off at exactly *start − N h*, and whether N can be a fraction; FR-33 L724 "show until" date) — *Fix:* "cancelling is allowed while now < start − N h; N is a whole number of hours"; "shown through the end of that date in the tenant's time zone".
- **[low] "Last-used language" for a patient who has never visited** (FR-28 L658; FR-43 L874–875) — A patient created by a practitioner booking (FR-14) has no last-used language, and FR-43's "remembered" could be per browser or stored on the patient, which SMS needs. *Fix:* store it on the patient and fall back to the tenant's default language.
- **[low] Loose ends** (NFR-3 L918 "API key" appears nowhere else; NFR-8 L930 still has `[NOTE FOR PM …]`; NFR-11/Q3 retention vs FR-42 L855 "no role can … delete", so a retention purge needs a defined path; FR-27 L643 "retried 5 times" could mean 5 or 6 sends, with no schedule) — *Fix:* remove or define the API key, drop the note, say that a retention purge is a separate privileged job (or defer it to Q3), and state the number of attempts and the backoff.
- **[low] The default daily OTP budget waits on Q1** (FR-3 L256–257) — The story can't meet "the default budget" without a value. *Fix:* give a placeholder default (configurable) until Q1 is answered.
- **[low] §7.2 orders epics against their dependencies** (§7.2 L1005–1012) — FR-10 (epic 2) and FR-15/FR-18 (epic 4) refer to reserved places, which arrive only in epic 6. *Fix:* move reserved places before booking core, or note that the earlier epics treat `reserved` as 0 until epic 6.
